                                       HOW TO PLAY
1. Find a path from Jim to the candy bucket. Wherever he is going, the bucket marks the
    finish.

2. You cannot walk through a wall.

3. Pick up as much candy as you can on the way.

4. Color in one box for every candy you get.

5. Write how many you got in the Total box.

6. See how close you came to the “Best possible” score.

7. Stuck? The answers are at the back.

8. The mazes get bigger as you go: 1 to 20 suit ages 6 and 7, 21 to 40 ages 8 and 9, and 41
    to 50 ages 10 and up.
